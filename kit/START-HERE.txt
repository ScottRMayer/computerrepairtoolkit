===========================================================
  PC REPAIR KIT
===========================================================

WHAT THIS IS
  A repair assistant on a USB drive. It looks at this computer,
  figures out what's wrong, and fixes what it safely can.


HOW TO USE IT
  1. Double-click            Repair-This-PC
  2. Click YES on the permission prompt.
     (If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click
      "More info" then "Run anyway" - this is your own drive.)
  3. Choose where to save a copy of this PC's files.
     An external drive is best. You can skip this, but read the
     warning it shows you first.
  4. Walk away. It usually takes 20 to 90 minutes.
     Leave the drive plugged in. Keep a laptop on its charger.
  5. When it's done, you'll get a summary of what it found,
     what it changed, and anything a person still needs to
     look at.


BEFORE YOU START
  - This computer needs to be online. The assistant does its
    thinking over the internet, so with no connection it will
    stop and tell you, rather than half-fixing things.
  - Nothing is deleted without a copy being kept first,
    wherever that's possible.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - To stop it: click the window and press Ctrl and C together.
    Don't just close the window with the X.
  - A complete record of everything it did is saved in the
    "logs" folder on this drive.
  - If it says it couldn't get online, the repair tools on this
    drive still work by hand. The exact commands are in
    docs\tool-invocations.md.


WHAT IT WON'T DO
  - It won't reinstall Windows or erase your disk.
  - It won't remove your accounts or your files.
  - Some fixes need a restart. It won't restart the computer
    on its own - it will tell you a restart is needed instead.
  - If it finds a failing hard drive or bad memory, it stops
    and tells you, because those need a person and new parts.
